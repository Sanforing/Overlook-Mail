What this is

A parody/educational web app. Every "email" in the inbox is actually a small browser app, novel, video, or game. Made for fun.

What this is not

This is not a real email service — you can't send or receive actual mail. Not a Microsoft product, not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Microsoft, Outlook, or Office 365 in any way.

Use it responsibly

Don't upload illegal or copyrighted content you don't own. Anything you mark "Public" can be seen by other users. We may remove content that breaks these rules.
